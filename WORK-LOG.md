# Work log

Copy this page into Notes, Google Docs, Word, or a notebook.
This is the record. The chat website is only the workshop.

One row per job. Add rows underneath the example. Delete the example row when you start.

| Date | Job | What I decided | Where the finished piece is | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-01-01 | Example: rewrite the volunteer email | Keep the short version | Doc "Volunteer email" | Send Friday |

## What to write down

- The decision, not the whole chat.
- The place the finished text lives (file name, doc link, or "printed and filed").
- The next step a future you, or a new chat, can act on.

Skip passwords, account numbers, and other secrets. The log can say "used the bank login" without the login.
